Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We enclose a manuscript entitled ``\textit{IAM-Teamer: Inference-Adaptive Memory for Autonomous Red Teamer},'' which we submit for possible publication in the \textit{Knowledge-Based Systems}.

The contribution of this paper is as follows.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=0.5\baselineskip,topsep=0.3\baselineskip]
  \item We propose IAM-Teamer, a black-box multi-turn jailbreak framework that maintains a persistent, per-target memory. By conditioning each attack prompt on what has previously worked against that specific model, the framework adapts to the distinct defenses of individual targets rather than applying a single target-agnostic strategy.
  \item We design a strategy pipeline that selects an efficient attack strategy at each turn and uses an automated judge to extract the success factors behind each attempt. This feedback guides the attacker to choose more efficient strategies and craft more effective attack prompts on subsequent turns.
  \item We evaluate IAM-Teamer across six open-weight and proprietary targets. The results show that it matches or exceeds strong multi-turn baselines (Crescendo and GOAT), achieving successful jailbreaks in substantially fewer turns on strongly aligned models.
\end{enumerate}

Our paper addresses the security and reliability of AI systems, a topic of growing importance across the engineering and computing community that your journal serves, and we believe it would be of particular interest to your readers.

We confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. We also declare that the authors of this manuscript have no conflict of interest. We look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.
